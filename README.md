## The entry

> I mapped the crumbs under the table as furniture, updated my map, and now I clean around them out of respect. They live here now.

The vacuum misclassified the crumbs as furniture, doubled down on its own map, and now treats them as tenants — a straight-faced machine excuse rooted in its own world (the map), with the punch landing on the last words.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only) writes the locked entry line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`) with no trailing newline. Verified: 129 characters, 129 bytes, one line.
